Keeping aircraft flight-ready

I am an aircraft maintenance technician (Cat. B1.1 – Mechanical) with hands-on experience maintaining Airbus (A319/320/321, A350) and Boeing (737, 777, 787) fleets. My work spans line maintenance, troubleshooting, scheduled and unscheduled inspections and component replacement, working from AMM, MEL, FIM and TSM to keep modern commercial aircraft airworthy.

I also maintain aircraft cabins: safety checks, snag rectification, seat repairs and galley and lavatory systems, keeping cabins ready through transit, pre-flight and night-stop operations.
